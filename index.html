Two-Column Comparator

Paste two Excel columns. Formatting is discarded and values are compared as text.
Original Excel row numbers are calculated from the pasted order, starting at row 1.
Blank pasted cells are ignored by default; row numbers refer to non-blank pasted values.
Example: AB.123 and AB/123 become comparable.
"All" makes spaces inside values irrelevant too.
